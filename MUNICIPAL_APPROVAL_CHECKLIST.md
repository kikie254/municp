# MUNICIPAL APPROVAL CHECKLIST

**For:** the authorised representative of Iten–Tambach Municipality
**Purpose:** items that must be confirmed, supplied or approved before this website is published as
an official municipal publication.
**How to use:** tick each line, sign the section, and return the completed sheet. Anything left
unticked keeps the site in demonstration status and blocks formal publication approval. Nothing on this list has been assumed,
invented or filled in on the Municipality's behalf.

Responsible municipal contact for this review: to be formally designated by the Municipality.

---

## 1. Official branding and identity

- [ ] Confirm the official name to be used site-wide: **"Iten–Tambach Municipality"** (current
      usage) or an alternative spelling.
- [ ] Confirm the official name of the parent authority: **"County Government of Elgeyo Marakwet"**.
- [ ] Confirm whether the County crest may be used on this website.
- [ ] Provide **written permission** for any crest use, or supply approved municipal artwork. The County crest has been removed from the demonstration branch pending written authorisation.
- [ ] Confirm the colour palette and typography are acceptable for institutional use.
- [ ] Confirm whether a different official logo exists for the Municipality itself.

## 2. Photography and image licensing

- [ ] Review the asset register in `ASSET_CREDITS.md` (13 photographs, all with unknown rights).
- [ ] For each proposed photograph, choose one: **supply licensed original** · **supply replacement owned by
      the Municipality** · **retain the neutral image placeholder**.
- [ ] Provide, for each retained image: photographer/creator, source, licence type, date obtained.
- [ ] Confirm in writing whether any current photograph shows a real municipal project, officer or
      event, and whether its use is authorised.
- [ ] Approve or reject continued use of images that associate the Municipality with a specific
      location, market or road.

> **Launch blocker.** The 13 previously used photographs had unknown rights and have been removed from the public assets. The site hides static and CMS-uploaded photographs unless `MUNICIPAL_IMAGE_ASSETS_APPROVED=true`. Supply licensed replacements and document web-use rights before enabling that flag.

## 3. Institutional information

- [ ] Confirm the municipal mandate wording and ensure the Urban Areas and Cities Act is distinguished from the model-draft charter currently available on the County website.
- [ ] Supply adopted vision and mission statements or approve the continued public wording that their status is pending confirmation.
- [ ] Confirm the relationship description with the County Government.
- [ ] Confirm the list of services the Municipality delivers directly, and those delivered by the
      County or national agencies.

## 4. Leadership and governance

- [ ] Confirm whether the **Iten Tambach Municipal Charter** published on the County site is the
      **adopted** instrument for this Municipality (the file supplied is headed "Model Draft
      Municipal Charter", World Bank KUSP).
- [ ] Supply current appointment instruments and verify the actual Board composition, appointment method, terms, Chairperson and Municipal Manager responsibilities against the adopted charter and applicable law.
- [ ] Decide whether office holders will be named and photographed. If yes, supply names, correct
      titles and approved portraits with consent.
- [ ] Approve the wording stating that no officials are currently named on the website.

- [ ] Approve continued availability of the 10 labelled template records as internal templates only

- [ ] Confirm whether notices must also appear in a specific legal format or gazette.
